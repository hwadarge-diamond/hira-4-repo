# 🏃‍♂️ RUN NETAJI RUN // European Tour 3D

> A hilarious, high-octane 3D endless runner built with **Three.js**, an elegant **Cream White & European Street** visual aesthetic, and 100% procedural synthesized audio using the **Web Audio API**.

---

## 🏛️ Game Overview & Story
Netaji, a charismatic cartoon Indian Politician dressed in his crisp white Khadi Kurta-Pyjama, Gandhi Topi, and Saffron Nehru waistcoat, is on an official diplomatic tour across Europe! While visiting historic capitals, an overly enthusiastic crowd of voters, supporters with garlands, and TV journalists with microphone booms spot him and start chasing him down the charming cobblestone boulevards.

Your mission: **RUN NETAJI RUN!** Dodge cafe bistro tables, slide under Parisian clotheslines, ride vintage Italian Vespas, collect golden Motichoor Laddoos, and power up with Cutting Chai!

---

## 🌟 Signature Features

1. **🏛️ Cream White European Cityscape**:
   - Quaint cobblestone streets, warm cream stone facades, Parisian mansard slate roofs, cafe awnings, blooming geranium flower boxes, and flying European pigeons.
2. **👥 The Chasing Public Crowd**:
   - Instead of a generic enemy, a lively 3D cartoon crowd of supporters, voters waving campaign signs, and eager TV journalists chases behind Netaji! If Netaji stumbles, the crowd swarms closer yelling for selfies and interviews!
3. **🧬 3 Cartoon Morphs**:
   - **🏃‍♂️ Netaji Sprint**: Classic brisk politician jog with waving hands, topi bob, and billowing kurta skirt!
   - **🛵 Vintage Vespa**: Netaji rides a cute pastel European scooter with a horn—glides smoothly over cafe bistro tables!
   - **🔮 Roly-Poly Netaji**: Netaji rolls into a round campaign sphere that squeezes easily under hanging laundry lines!
4. **☕ Cutting Chai Turbo (Stamina Bullet-Time)**:
   - Drink steaming cutting chai to slow time to 0.35x speed, weaving effortlessly through dense crowds with golden marigold trails!
5. **🔄 Policy U-Turn (Rooftop Gravity Flip)**:
   - Step on golden campaign pads (`[F]` / `[G]`) to sprint upside-down along historic European rooftops!
6. **🍬 Desi Campaign Collectibles**:
   - **Golden Motichoor Laddoos** (Currency)
   - **Z-Plus Security Shield** (Absorbs 1 obstacle impact)
   - **Voter Magnet** (Draws all laddoos toward Netaji)
   - **Double Mandate 2x** (Doubles score)
   - **Chai & Samosa Turbo** (Supersonic invincible sprint)
7. **👔 Netaji's Wardrobe (Shop)**:
   - Unlock customizable Nehru waistcoats: *Saffron Khadi*, *Royal Blue Silk*, *Grassroots Green*, *Maroon Velvet*, and *Maharaja Gold Zari*!

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Desktop Key | Mobile Gesture |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Move Left / Right** | `A` / `D` or `◄` / `►` | Swipe Left / Right |
| **Jump** | `W` / `Space` / `▲` | Swipe Up or **JUMP** Button |
| **Slide / Drop** | `S` / `▼` | Swipe Down or **SLIDE** Button |
| **Policy Flip (Rooftops)** | `F` or `G` | Rooftop Chevron Pads |
| **Morph Form** | `1` (Sprint), `2` (Vespa), `3` (Roly-Poly) | On-Screen Form Buttons |
| **Cutting Chai Turbo** | Hold `Shift` | Tap & Hold **CHAI** Button |
| **Pause** | `P` or `Escape` | Pause Button |
